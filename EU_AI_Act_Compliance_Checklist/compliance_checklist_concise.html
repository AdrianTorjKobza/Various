EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) Readiness Checklist

Act Summary

The EU AI Act is the world's first comprehensive horizontal legal framework for AI. It follows a risk-based approach, imposing stricter rules on systems with higher potential to harm society. The Act applies to Providers (developers) and Deployers (users) of AI systems in the EU market.

Official Documentation: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689
