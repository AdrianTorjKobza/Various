About Overlap Cities

Outlines keep their real-world size wherever you drag them: each city is re-projected around its new position, so the comparison stays accurate even when moving between the equator and high latitudes.

Which cities are included

Every city with 300,000+ inhabitants, plus the world's top 100 city destinations – some of which are smaller.

How "city limits" are defined

Normally the official city boundary. Where that boundary is province-sized or mostly rural (e.g. Beijing, Istanbul, Dubai), a narrower definition is used – such as the central districts or the urbanised part. Each city's definition is shown under City facts.

Privacy

No accounts, no cookies, no tracking. Your last two cities are stored only in this browser. To show the weather, a city's coordinates (never anything about you) are sent to Open-Meteo.
